# First Month Launch Strategy

A simple plan to launch the LEGO mosaic kits with a small, safe budget.

**Money note:** 1 EUR ≈ 3.333 TND (based on 3,000 EUR = 10,000 TND).

**Real price confirmed on Alibaba: 32.461 TND/kg** for 1×1 pixel-art plates.
Confirmed from the listing: sold **per single color**, **minimum 1 kg**, and they have
**~82 colors** (covers our 33). Bigger orders are cheaper:

| Order size | Price per kg |
|---|--:|
| **1–99 kg** (us — 33 kg) | **32.461 TND** |
| 100–999 kg | 30.986 TND |
| 1000 kg+ | 26.559 TND |

---

## 1. The plan in one line

Launch with **one size (32×32)**, **only 1×1 parts**, in **all 33 colors**, buying
**1 kg of each color (33 kg total)**. Upfront cost: **~2,000 TND** — only one-fifth
of the 10,000 TND budget.

---

## 2. The product

| Choice | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Size | **32×32 only** (about 25 cm, ~1,024 pieces) | Bigger sizes need far more plastic and break the budget. Turn off the larger sizes for now. |
| Parts | **1×1 plates only** (BrickLink part 3024) | Every picture can be built from 1×1 plates. Only 33 things to buy, not hundreds. Bigger bricks can be added later. |
| Colors | **All 33 colors** | You can't predict which colors a customer's photo will need, so you must keep them all. Cutting colors would break some pictures. |

---

## 3. The stock to buy

Buy **1 kg of every color** = **33 kg total**.

- 1 kg ≈ 5,000 pieces (a 1×1 plate weighs about 0.2 g).
- 33 kg ≈ **165,000 pieces** ≈ **~160 kits** of plastic.
- This is far more than 20 orders — so the plastic lasts you **many months**, not just one.

The full color list to send to sellers is in **[palette.csv](palette.csv)**.

---

## 4. The cost (at the real 32 TND/kg price)

| Item | Cost | Note |
|---|--:|---|
| Plastic — 33 kg (1 kg × 33 colors) | **1,056 TND** | 32 TND/kg, one-time buy |
| Shipping to Tunisia | ~500 TND | estimate — confirm with seller |
| Packaging (20 kits)\* | ~400 TND | baseplates + boxes + manuals + packing |
| **TOTAL** | **~2,000 TND** | budget is 10,000 TND |

\*Packaging = baseplates + boxes + printed manuals + packing, for the first 20 kits.

- The **plastic (33 kg)** is a one-time buy that lasts **~160 kits** (many months).
- The **packaging** is per batch of kits — you re-buy it as you sell more.
- **You use only ~1/5 of the budget** — plenty left for more stock or marketing.

---

## 5. Price and profit per kit

| | Amount |
|---|---|
| Plastic in one kit (~0.2 kg) | ~7 TND |
| Baseplate + box + manual + packing | ~20 TND |
| **Cost to make one 32×32 kit** | **~27 TND** |
| Your **selling price** | **80–110 TND** |
| **Profit per kit** | **~53–83 TND** |

Your target price is both **affordable for customers** and **very profitable**.

If you sell all 20 kits in month 1 at ~95 TND: about **1,900 TND revenue** for ~530 TND
of cost (20 kits) — and you still have **~140 kits of plastic left** for the next months.

---

## 7. Checklist before launch

- [ ] Ask the seller for their **full color chart** (all ~82 colors with photos/codes).
- [ ] **Match our 33 colors to their color codes** (B01, A13…). *Best option:* get their
      real colors and use them in the app, so the screen preview matches the real bricks.
- [ ] Confirm **shipping to Tunisia** + any **import tax**.
- [ ] Order 33 kg (1 kg × 33 chosen colors) — ~1,071 TND at 32.461 TND/kg.
- [ ] Buy 20 baseplates (32×32), boxes, and print 20 manuals.
- [ ] In the app: turn off the bigger sizes, keep **32×32**.
- [ ] In the app: set output to **1×1 parts only** for now.
- [ ] Set the selling price (suggest ~95–110 TND).

---

## 8. Notes for later (after month 1)

- Add **bigger bricks** (1×2, 2×2…) to use fewer pieces per kit.
- Add **bigger sizes** (48×48) once cash flow is stable.
- Watch which colors get used most, and reorder those in bigger amounts.
